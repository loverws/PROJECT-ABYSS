# Roblox Mobile Controls

Keep move, look, fire, reload, dash and weapon swap reachable during simultaneous actions.
Parameterize sensitivity and aim assistance; never turn assistance into target locking.
Use device emulation for layout coverage, not as proof of real-phone feel or performance.
Record screen, orientation, reachability, readability, sensitivity, frame time and memory results.
Do not freeze physical constants until the owner completes the real-phone Human Gate.
